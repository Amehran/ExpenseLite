Offline-First & Zero Telemetry

Privacy Policy for ExpenseLite

Effective Date: September 18, 2026 | Last Updated: September 18, 2026

Thank you for using ExpenseLite ("the App"). Your privacy is our top priority. ExpenseLite is built from the ground up as an offline-first, zero-telemetry personal finance tracker. This policy explains how data is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

ExpenseLite does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, sensitive user information, or device identifiers.

  • No User Account Required: You do not need to register, create an account, or log in.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: We do not track user behavior, usage metrics, clickstream data, or performance analytics.
  • No Third-Party Trackers or Advertising: The App contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking tools.
  • No Server Connections: Your data is never sent to any remote server or cloud infrastructure.

2. On-Device Local Data Storage

All financial records, expense entries, income logs, and category settings created within ExpenseLite are stored strictly on your local device using secure local database storage.

  • Data Control: Your data never leaves your device unless you manually choose to export it.
  • Data Export & Import: If you use the JSON Data Export feature, backup files are generated locally under your explicit control.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete your data at any time by deleting individual entries inside the App, clearing App storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the application.

3. App Permissions

ExpenseLite operates with minimal system permissions:

  • No Network Permission Required: ExpenseLite does not request or require internet access for core financial tracking.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: We do not request access to your location, camera, contacts, microphone, SMS, or phone storage.

4. Children's Privacy

ExpenseLite does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or 16 in specific jurisdictions). The App is safe for general audiences.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory guidelines. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
