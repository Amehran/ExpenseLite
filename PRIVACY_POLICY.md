# Privacy Policy for ExpenseLite

**Effective Date:** September 18, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 18, 2026  

Thank you for using **ExpenseLite** ("the App"). Your privacy is our top priority. ExpenseLite is designed from the ground up as an **offline-first, zero-telemetry personal finance tracker**. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Zero Personal Data Collection

ExpenseLite does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, sensitive user information, or device identifiers. 

- **No User Account Required:** You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to use ExpenseLite.
- **No Analytics / Telemetry:** We do not track user behavior, usage metrics, clickstream data, or performance telemetry.
- **No Third-Party Trackers or Advertising:** The App contains **no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking technologies**.
- **No Server Connections:** The App does not send your data to any remote server or cloud infrastructure.

---

## 2. On-Device Local Data Storage

All financial records, expense entries, income logs, and custom category data created within ExpenseLite are stored **strictly on your local device** using encrypted local SQLite database storage.

- **Data Control:** Your financial data never leaves your device unless you manually choose to export it.
- **Data Export & Import:** If you utilize the JSON Data Export feature in ExpenseLite, the backup file is created locally on your device under your explicit control.
- **Data Deletion:** You can delete your data at any time by deleting individual transactions within the App, clearing the App’s storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the App.

---

## 3. App Permissions

ExpenseLite operates with minimal system permissions:
- **No Internet Permission Required:** ExpenseLite does not request network access permissions for core financial tracking.
- **No Sensitive Permissions:** ExpenseLite does not request access to your location, camera, contacts, microphone, SMS, or phone storage.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

ExpenseLite does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain legal jurisdictions). The App is safe for general audiences.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect updates in app functionality or regulatory guidelines. Any changes will be updated on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
